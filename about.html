How we think about formulas

Fewer, more deliberate ingredients.

01

Start with the skin concern

Every formula answers one real concern — dullness and early signs of aging, or puffiness and dark circles — instead of trying to do everything at once.

02

Choose gentle over aggressive

We reach for ingredients like bakuchiol instead of harsher actives, so formulas stay usable for sensitive and reactive skin.

03

Keep it fragrance-free

No added fragrance in our serum, so there's one less common irritant to worry about on sensitive skin.
